# garner design system

Light and dark, following the system. Flat surfaces, hairline borders, no gradients but a category hue. The one translucent surface is the desktop sidebar, and macOS draws it, not CSS. Hierarchy comes from size, weight and the two text colors. Sentence case everywhere, except native macOS menus, which take Apple's title case.

All tokens live in `src/ui/tokens.css`. The whole Tailwind default theme is wiped there (`--*: initial`), so a theme-driven class outside this list produces no CSS. `bun check` fails on arbitrary values (`text-[13px]`, `bg-[#fff]`), inline `style`, `rounded-full`/`rounded-none` and gradients (`design.grit`); brackets are only for state selectors (`data-[status=active]`) and Base UI CSS variables.

Dark mode (2026-10-11) lives in the tokens alone: every color token is a `light-dark()` pair, light first, and the only other pairs are `text-tile` and the glass recipe. Components never know the mode: no `dark:` variant, no script reads it. A document opts in with `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` (`index.html`); without it `light-dark()` stays light, so the MCP card is light until it follows its host. Both builds follow the system: the desktop window sets no `theme`, so AppKit's appearance, the sidebar material and the web view's `prefers-color-scheme` follow it live. The dark values are macOS 27 darkAqua as drawn, measured off screen: the page is the window background, a block is the System Settings group box a step lighter, lines are the system separator, text is the label colors as drawn, and the tones keep their own hue at the lightness of Apple's dark ones. The category hues are single values in both modes: `MeshCard` paints them through an `OffscreenCanvas`, which rejects `light-dark()` and would draw black. Both Vite configs build CSS for `es2025`: at Vite's default target, Safari 16.4, Lightning CSS rewrites `light-dark()` into variables nothing sets and every token computes transparent in the build, light too, while development never shows it.

|---|---|

| Shadow | `shadow-popover`, for floating layers only, three times stronger in dark |

| Motion | Plain `transition`: 120ms, one easing. Buttons scale to 0.97 on press. The one exception is the `Glass` press, measured from native: 150ms `ease-out`, grows to 1.04. `animate-reveal` wipes a chart in from the left over 900ms. The `Drop` cards fan out over 500ms, then float and sway (`drop.css`). Scale, reveal and drop effects sit behind `motion-safe` or stop under reduced motion |

## Rules

- Never add a token, size, weight, radius or color without a decision. Extend `tokens.css`, never a component.
- Hover is always `bg-fill`. The exceptions are the primary button, `bg-ink/85`, and sidebar rows and `IconGrid` tiles: an unselected one takes `bg-fill/50` on hover and the selected one keeps `bg-fill`. The only other opacity modifiers are the `Palette` panel, `bg-sidebar/75`, a `Notice`, `bg-surface/90`, the sticky header of a `DataTable`, `bg-surface/75`, the window bar and the drop overlay, `bg-canvas/75`, a `Glass` divider and press, `ink` at 25% and 12%, a disabled `Glass` segment, `text-ink/25`, and a `Notice`'s rim, `border-ink/8`.
- Files dragged over the window show `Drop` (`src/ui/drop.tsx`, Dmytro's pick among five looks on Kit, 2026-10-17, replacing a disc ringed by its label set on a circle): the whole app under `bg-canvas/75` and a `blur-veil` backdrop blur, and in the middle a hand of three statement cards (72 by 96, `surface` with a `line` hairline, `rounded-control` and the `popover` shadow, each a logo slot over four rows of a 10px tile and a `fill` line) over a `text-heading` medium title and one `dim` line, Bank statements in CSV or Excel. The front card's tiles are solid hues, the back cards' are `fill`. On an institution page, or a manual account's page, the drop imports into that institution: the title reads Import into and its name, and its `Glyph` fills the front card's logo slot; elsewhere the title is Drop to import. As it opens the back cards fan from 4 to 10 degrees about a point far below the hand over 500ms; while open the hand floats 3px over 4.8s and the back cards sway 2 degrees further out and 2px up in turn over 3.6s (`drop.css`), all still under reduced motion. It never takes pointer events, is hidden from assistive tech, blurs and moves only while open, and does not show over an open prompt, where a drop is refused.
- The desktop build marks `<html>` with `data-desktop`, once, in `src/main.tsx`. `in-data-desktop:` is the only platform variant and it only removes a background: `body`, the `Sidebar` and the pending sidebar column turn transparent there, and the window's `sidebar` material shows through. Everything else paints its own background: the page column, the failure screen and the pending page side are `bg-canvas`, and any new full-window screen must be too.
- Primitives in `src/ui/` take typed props only, no `className`. `Button` is `variant: primary | secondary | ghost` and `size: sm | md` (28 and 32px). Primary and secondary are medium weight, ghost is regular and `dim`.
- New primitives start from the shadcn Base UI source, then get rewritten to these tokens and stripped to what is used.
- Every block on a page (table, settings group, `Card`) is one surface: `rounded-surface border border-line bg-surface`, a step lighter than the page in both modes, with a hairline border. Rows inside are split by `line` hairlines. Floating layers (dialogs, menus, tooltips) use the same surface plus `shadow-popover`.
- Lists of records render through `Table`. The Institutions table (Home and Crypto) leads each row with the institution's `Glyph` before its name, like the sidebar (2026-10-12). The card has the same 8px padding on all four sides. Inside it, a `dim` caption header and 40px rows share one grid: the first column takes the spare width, every other column is 112px and right-aligned. A `line` hairline sits above every row, inset to the text. A row with a destination is one link across its whole width and fills on hover. Row actions sit in their own layer at the right end, appear on hover or keyboard focus, and stay visible while their menu is open. A titled block of page content is a `Section`.
- Every balance renders through `Money` or `Amount` (`src/routes/-money.tsx`), never through a formatter directly. `Money` converts USD to the display currency. In privacy mode each renders `Mask`, a fixed blurred placeholder, so no real digit reaches the DOM. Prices are public and stay visible. `Amount` takes the asset id: a currency prints as money in its own currency, to the cent, anything else as a quantity.
- A key number is a `Stat`: a `dim` label over a `text-title` value. A two-tone table cell is a `Label`: medium text plus a `dim` note.
- Settings follow the Cursor settings page: a narrow `Page`, then `SettingsGroup` blocks. A group is a `dim` label over a block, with an optional `dim` note under it. Each `SettingsRow` has an `ink` title over a `dim` description on the left and one control on the right, rows are split by a `line` hairline inset to the text. A choice control is a `Picker` (`src/ui/picker.tsx`): a secondary `sm` button showing the current value with a chevron, opening a `Menu`. An on or off control is a `Switch`: a 28 by 16 track, `shade` when off and `positive` when on, with a `knob` thumb.
- The command palette is `Palette`: a 600px panel in the `sidebar` color at 75% over a `blur-glass` backdrop blur, anchored 80px from the top so it never jumps, with a 48px `text-nav` input row, a hairline, then grouped rows built like sidebar rows (34px, the icon in a 20px slot so a `Glyph`, a `TokenIcon` and a category's `sm` `Tile` align, label, `dim` note, `dim` value at the right end, which never shrinks while the label and the note truncate). The highlighted row is `bg-fill`, the first row is always highlighted and Enter runs it, and digits always type (28a dropped the keys 1 to 5 that ran the first five rows: they made amounts and years untypable). It matches through `search` (`search.md`): a blank query lists the groups in their order, five rows each; while typing, Merchants and Categories join, every group shows at most five rows, and groups follow their best match. The input leads with the search icon in the same 20px slot. While typing, a Movements group (`useFind` in `-search.tsx`) joins: a reading row (the search icon, the query read back as one sentence such as `Spent matching "groceries" in September 2026`, at its right end the count, then the total, Spent, Received or Net, and its `Money`), a words row when a one-word filter also matches as text, then the three newest matching movements (their table tile, the merchant or the bank text, the day and the account as the note, the amount at the right). It ranks as a note match and leads whenever the query holds a period, an amount or a flag; stop words alone show none; Enter on any of its rows opens the results page. A key hint is a `Kbd`, an 18px `fill` caption pill (the search field's ⌘K hint). Closing it never hands focus back, so no focus ring is left on the row that opened it. It opens with cmd+K or the window bar's search field, whose key hint is a `fill` caption pill shown on hover.
- The results page, `/movements?q=` (`movements.tsx`, prototyped in 28b), lists the movements a query matches: the reading as its title, Edit search (a secondary `sm` button) reopening the palette with the text selected, `Summary` with the total first (Spent, Received or Net), then `Movements` with an Account column and nothing folded. It has no field of its own, so the one search stays the palette (23p). Without `q` it lists every movement; Go to's Movements opens it.
- A message about the app's state that waits for the user is a `Notice` (`src/ui/notice.tsx`): `surface` at 90% over a `blur-bar` backdrop blur with a `line` hairline and the `popover` shadow, so it reads as a layer above a card's rows (both 2026-10-12, it blended into the Institutions table at 75% with no shadow), `rounded-glass` with 12px of padding on every side (2026-10-12), an icon in its tone's color (`dim`, `positive`, `warning` or `negative`; the tone colors nothing else) or a `lead` of its own, a medium title over an optional `dim` line, then an optional action and an optional ghost close. Its words are as few as possible: a title of a few words, at most one short line. The one in use asks to categorize (reworded 2026-10-12, Dmytro's pick on Kit): the `Scan` as its lead, "Let your AI app categorize 120 movements", "Copy the prompt, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, done.", and a secondary `Copy` button that reads Copy prompt, then Copied for 1.5s; it shows on Home, Fiat, an institution page with imports and an account page with movements, and closing it holds until more movements need a category. The other asks to confirm transfers (2026-10-12, `useTransfersNotice` in `-flows.tsx`): `Match` as its lead, "3 transfers between your accounts to confirm", "They count as spending until confirmed." (reworded 2026-10-12, his pick among four: the line says why it matters, a proposal stays in the spending totals until a decision), a secondary Review button that goes to Home's Transfers to confirm section, and closing it holds until more proposals appear. A page holds a stack of them, through `Page.notices` (`Notices` in `src/ui/notices.tsx`, 2026-10-12): `useNotices` in `-notices.tsx` lists the ones that apply, each under a key, and the stack keeps them in arrival order, 12px apart, so a notice that appears later joins below the ones already there and the others never move. One that appears folds open from nothing over 250ms (`fold` in `notices.css`: a grid row from `0fr` to `1fr` with opacity and an 8px rise, nothing under reduced motion), one that goes folds shut the same way and leaves the tree 300ms later, its content kept until then; the stack renders nothing when it is empty. Each notice's dismissal is kept under its key (`-notice.ts`), so closing one never closes another. The stack floats at the bottom of the page column above every sticky header, centered and as wide as a narrow page (704px), 24px above the window's edge, on short pages too, and keeps its place in the flow, so the end of the page is never under it; that place is 24px above the page's end as well (a negative margin against the page's 48px bottom padding), so it never shifts when the page is scrolled to its end (2026-10-12). `Scan` (`src/ui/scan.tsx`, `scan.css`, prototyped on Kit 2026-10-12) is the categorizing picture: a `rounded-control` sheet of five rows, each a `rounded-tile` category box and a text line, 36 by 44 in a notice or 96 by 120; a beam, 2px or 4px, sweeps down over 60% of a 3.6s cycle painted with the tile gradient of the row it is on and glowing in its hue, the colour easing to the next row's as it travels (a registered `--hue` property, `hues.css`, animated between the five hues), and as it passes each row the box fades in and the line flashes in the hue, both settling with one gentle rise to 1.04, the line resting in a 30% tint; at 84% the sheet clears and it starts over. Under reduced motion it stands still, every row identified. Decided with Dmytro: the beam over a soft band, bolder and coloured rather than a hairline, the smaller boxes so the rows breathe, one motion for box and line.
- The pair sheet (`PairSheet` in `-pair.tsx`, Dmytro's pick among three looks on Kit, 2026-10-12) is the receipt a Transfers to confirm row opens: titled Pair two movements over Mark as a transfer between own accounts, it shows the two accounts facing each other, each its institution glyph and name with Lucide's ArrowRight between them, the amount sent in `text-title` on a dotted hairline, then Date (one day, or the sending day to the arrival date when they differ) and, for a short pair, Received and Fee, a one-line Always pair similar transfers switch, and a ghost Cancel beside the primary Pair. Every section sits 24px from the next, the subtitle and the buttons included (a `gap-6` column with 12px of its own padding over the bars' 12px). It carries no memos and no times of day: the row is the match, the sheet is the receipt. A `DataTable` row opens it through `onOpen`, the row as a button with the link look. `Match` (`src/ui/match.tsx`, `match.css`, Dmytro's pick among nine motions on Kit, 2026-10-12) is the transfers picture, 44 by 44: two small ledger sheets (`rounded-chip`, a `line` hairline, three `fill` rows) at the ends of a `rounded-control` sheet, the middle row of each in the `brass` tile gradient; over a 3s cycle the two brass rows brighten from 35% and rise to 1.08 together, then a 12px `green` tick tile springs in between them at 55% and holds until 84%, when everything clears and it starts over. Under reduced motion the rows and the tick stand lit.
- A `Section` may carry one action at the right of its title, a ghost `sm` button (Remove AI answers on Rules).
- A chart or any other standalone block sits in a `Card`: a block with 20px padding, an optional `ink` title over an optional `dim` description, then the content. A card that opens with a `Stat` has no title: the stat's label heads it, as in the History card.
- A category has a hue, one of 17 set by `data-hue` from `src/ui/hues.css` (`Hue` and `color` in `src/ui/hues.ts`, each a `--hue-<name>` variable). The hue is the vivid color, for charts. A `Tile` is one diagonal gradient of its hue, set once on `[data-hue]` as `--tile-from` and `--tile-to`: 90% of the chroma, 0.08 lighter and turned 20 degrees back at the top left, 0.06 darker and 20 degrees on at the bottom right (picked 2026-10-06 over flat, glossy and darker treatments). `variant: solid` paints it opaque (`bg-tile-solid`) under a white icon at 90% in both modes; `soft`, the default, paints the same two colors at 20% (`bg-tile`) under the icon in `text-tile`, OKLCH lightness 0.56 for every hue, 0.7 in dark, so every icon has the same contrast in both modes. The only other gradient is the `MeshCard` glare. It is `rounded-tile`, its icon about 60% of it: `sm` 20px with a 14px icon, `md` 32px with 20px, `lg` 48px with 28px. A `BlendChart` (`src/ui/charts/blend.tsx`) draws one bar per column, at most 32px wide with 6px corners, filled bottom to top with every slice in palette order, so neighbors are related hues and the order holds across bars: each slice is a solid band of its vivid hue as tall as its share, and the seam between two bands blends over 80% of the smaller one; the tooltip lists every slice, largest first. A `MeshCard` (`src/ui/mesh-card.tsx`, styles in `mesh-card.css`) is a card-shaped block (`aspect-8/5`, `rounded-surface`, the `line` border of every surface, no shadow; the shader and the glare are cut by `clip-surface`, a `clip-path` at the radius inside the border, since WebKit leaves the canvas of a tilted block square under `overflow-hidden` and a clip on the block itself cuts its border) painted with Paper's mesh gradient (distortion 0.8, swirl 0.1), drifting at speed 0.2 and still under reduced motion. Each card starts at its own frame and rotation, both from a hash of the account's name, so cards differ and one card always looks the same. Its `face`, the glyph and the balance with the name, is optional: without it the card is the gradient alone, as Kit's Every hue shows ten hues around the wheel, the most the mesh takes (2026-10-07). Its 10 color spots are split between the categories of at least 5% of the total, by largest remainder, in palette order: each is the vivid hue read from its `--hue-<name>` and drawn to sRGB on a 1px canvas, since the shader takes only hex, rgb and hsl, fully saturated. The institution's `Glyph` (32px) and the balance in `text-heading` sit at the top, the account's name in `text-heading` at the bottom left; the card carries no garner mark (picked 2026-10-06 over the mark top left with the glyph where a card network's logo sits). All are `etched`: white at 75% in overlay, so they take the tint of the colors under them, like laser etching (picked 2026-10-06 over hairline, foil and deboss; texture and chip were tried and left out). The pointer tilts the card up to 7 degrees on one axis and 9 on the other (`tilt`, behind `motion-safe`) and lifts a faint white `glare` around it; its position reaches CSS as `--x` and `--y`, written on the element by the card's pointer handlers, one of the two places a script sets a style, the other being the `Scroller` spacer's height. A category's hue follows its icon (`hues` in `src/routes/-categories.tsx`, one per `CategoryIcon` key), so a category of the user's own gets one with no migration; a hue of its own, picked by the user, would need a column (2026-10-06).
- A long, sortable list is a `DataTable` (`src/ui/data-table.tsx`), prototyped on Kit (2026-10-06) beside `Table`. Columns are our own type (`id`, `header`, `cell`, an optional `sort` key, a `width` of `fill`, `sm` 80px, `md` 112px or `lg` 160px, `end` for right alignment); TanStack Table sorts; the rows are plain flow, never virtualized, since a list shows 50 at a time (virtualization was tried and dropped on 2026-10-06: it cost a scroll margin, spacers written by a script and a scroll anchoring loop, and it could not keep a month header pinned). The rows scroll with the page, never in a box of their own (one scroll, the whole window of rows, Page Down and Home work), and the header sticks under the window bar, 52px down, in `surface` at 75% over a `blur-bar` backdrop blur. A sortable header is a button: `dim`, `ink` on hover and while sorted, with a 12px Lucide arrow for the direction (`ArrowUp`, `ArrowDown`, the one place icons are not Hugeicons, picked 2026-10-06); numbers sort largest first, text A to Z. Rows keep the `Table` look (rounded `fill` hover, `line` hairlines inset to the text) in three densities for different places: `comfortable` 40px (a 20px category `Tile` and a `Label`), `compact` 32px and `dense` 28px in `text-caption`, both without icons (a 48px two-line density was tried and left out). A long list never shows everything at once: it opens with the first 50 rows of its sorted order, and a full-width row under them, Show 50 more (medium) with the `dim` count beside it (100 of 10,000), `dim` and `ink` with `fill` on hover, adds the next 50; sorting always covers every row. A `fold` (`when`, `label`) takes matching rows out of the list: each group, or the whole list when flat, carries one row under its header, built like a row (a 20px `fill` tile holding a Lucide `ChevronDown`, `ChevronUp` once open, and the label in medium: 12 transfers), that opens and closes them in place, and Show more counts only the rows shown; movements fold the categories of type `transfer`. A movement's amount above zero is in `positive`, the value only; everything else in the row stays `ink`. A `group` (`by` a column, `of` a key, `head` the header) makes each run of rows that share a key a block of its own under a 64px header row, a `line` hairline above it, that sticks under the column header (84px down, the same `surface` at 75% and `blur-bar`) until the next block pushes it away, only while the list is sorted by that column, so a list sorted by amount is flat; the header gets every row of its group, not only those shown. Prototyped on Kit (2026-10-06), Movements by month: a movement leads with its category's 20px `Tile`, its text is the merchant (the bank text in the `dim` note), the Category column is the name alone (`dim` while it is the AI's answer), the date is the day alone; each month header holds the month in medium, Spent, In and Out as `dim` labels before their amounts, and at its right end a `Ring` (`src/ui/charts/ring.tsx`), a 40px donut of the month's spending by category in their vivid hues, largest first from twelve o'clock, each arc exactly in proportion to its amount (the small gaps are taken from the circle before it is shared), each segment a `Tooltip` naming its category and share, the whole ring labelled for screen readers.
- Charts are the primitives in `src/ui/charts/`, always inside a `Card`, 256px tall, or with `fit` as tall as the card around them. Series are `ink` and lighter mixes of it, never hues, except in a `BlendChart`. Lines are 1.5px and monotone over time, framed by faint marks: dotted `line` hairlines at up to four values and at the date marks, month names under the plot (calendar aligned every 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 months, then every 2, 5 or 10 years, at most five; a January shows its year, fewer than two month starts show days, a mark too close to the start is dropped), and compact values on the right with as many digits as the spread needs, left out when the line is flat and in privacy mode, all in `text-mark` `faint`. A line chart reaches 12px into the card's right and bottom padding. The hover tooltip carries the full date and the value. Bars keep their category labels. Fills are flat textures (dots that shrink toward the bottom, or a hairline hatch), never gradients or glows. A legend shows only with two or more series, and clicking an entry isolates it. Styling of Recharts internals lives in `src/ui/charts/charts.css`, nowhere else. `SankeyChart` (`src/ui/charts/sankey.tsx`, 2026-10-13) is the third surface in hues after `BlendChart` and `Ring`, with a layered layout of its own over nodes and links, no library: depth by longest path, sinks in the last column, every column centred, each column ordered by its neighbours' positions with the leftover pinned last; 8px bars with 12px gaps (28 under a labelled bar), filled ribbons at 35% (65% lit, 12% dimmed, 120ms) in the hue of their hued end, brass between two accounts, gray into Kept; caption labels with compact amounts in 136px gutters, none in privacy mode, a label only on a bar of 4px or more, names cut at 16 characters; a middle bar's name under it with a surface halo; hover and the tooltip (share of the side, the rows of the other end, In and Out for a middle bar) through Base UI tooltips opened at once, the lit state following the open tooltip.
- A value to copy gets a `Copy` ghost button that shows a tick for a moment; with `variant: secondary` it carries its label and reads Copied for 1.5s, its width easing between the two over 200ms (`fluid` on `Button` transitions `width`; `Copy` pins the measured width before the swap, measures the new one and eases to it, then lets the width go, since a content change never moves the computed `auto`, 2026-10-12).
- A token shows its logo through `TokenIcon`, a canvas tile in `rounded-control`, 20px in tables, palette rows and cards with the logo inset 2px, 28px next to a page title inset 3px. The tile takes the logo's own background color, the one most of eight points near its edge agree on, so the color fills the tile and the glyph gets room. A logo without a solid background sits on `fill`. The page resolves one known deployment of the coin (natives through the zero address) and asks the host port for the bytes with `icon(coin, source)`, painted from a blob URL: the host fetches the logo once from the DefiLlama icon CDN at 96px and caches it per coingecko id (`data/icons/` in the bun host, the app cache folder on the desktop), an empty file remembering a 404 or 410, so every coin in the token universe has a logo and the page never loads from a CDN. An unknown token shows its first letter in a `fill` tile. A currency with a bundled flag (`src/ui/flags/<code>.svg`, square flags from flag-icons 7.5, MIT: EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, JPY, CAD, AUD, PLN, SEK, NOK, DKK, CZK) shows the flag edge to edge in the same tile, with no canvas; any other currency shows its first letter.
- An empty value is a plain hyphen. Relative times are short: `just now`, `6m ago`, `3h ago`, `2d ago`.
- The sidebar is separated from the page by color only, never a border: `sidebar` against `canvas` in the browser, the window material against `canvas` on the desktop. The sidebar is 209px, set while the `Toolbar` still held Back and Forward and kept. Its 52px strip and its footer stay put and the rows between them scroll, in a scroller that reaches 8px past the rows' sides and 4px above the first and below the last, so the focus ring is not clipped. Nav rows are 32px with 15px padding on the left and 10px on the right, a 10px icon gap and no space between rows, so there is no dead zone between two rows; the section labels keep 10px. Sidebar text is `text-nav` medium and `dim`; the active row is `bg-fill` and turns `ink`, and its icon follows. A page below a row keeps that row active: an account its institution, a merchant or a category Categories, an asset Crypto or Fiat by its kind (`current` on `SidebarLink`, which sets `aria-current` like an active link, 2026-10-07). An unselected row takes `bg-fill/50` on hover. Icons inside the sidebar are 15px.
