# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Crypto on 7 EVM chains from watch-only addresses, and manual holdings, with daily value history.
- Bank statement imports from Revolut, Santander and ActivoBank into one ledger of movements.
- Categories and merchants, set by hand or by your own AI app through Garner's MCP server.
- Transfers between your own accounts, paired on their own or confirmed one by one.
- Spending by month, money flow and net worth history, in USD or EUR, with privacy mode.
- A native macOS app: light and dark, native menus with SF Symbols, the menu bar.
